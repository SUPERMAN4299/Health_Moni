About the Project

This project aims to create a cutting-edge health monitoring system that provides users with real-time health data and personalized insights. The system integrates advanced sensors, AI-driven analytics, and a user-friendly interface to empower individuals to take control of their health and well-being.

Key Features

Heart Rate Monitoring

Track your heart rate in real-time and identify potential anomalies.

Live Prescription from AI

Receive AI-generated health advice and prescriptions based on your live data.

Live GSR value for tracking mood

Monitor stress, anxiety, and depression levels through Galvanic Skin Response.

Live Temperature Data

Get real-time body temperature readings for instant health checks.

High Security of Data

Your health data is protected with end-to-end encryption and advanced security protocols.

Live Environment Analysis

Monitor air quality, humidity, and other environmental factors affecting your health.
